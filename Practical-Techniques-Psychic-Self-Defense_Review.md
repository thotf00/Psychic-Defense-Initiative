# Practical Techniques for Psychic Defense: A Comprehensive Manual for the Psychic Defense Initiative

## Abstract

The Psychic Defense Initiative emerges in response to a convergence of factors that have accelerated humanity's exposure to psychic and astral dimensions. Individuals gain access to Out of Body Experiences (OBE), astral travel, and psi capacities through various means: trauma response, dreaming, meditation, drug use, and genetic predisposition. This expansion of consciousness, while potentially transformative, occurs without corresponding education about the risks, protocols, and defensive measures necessary for safe navigation.

Murry Hope's work, written in the late 20th century, anticipated this phenomenon through her description of the "Aquarian Age Shift"—a period of reawakening right-brain function leading to widespread spiritual exploration and, consequently, potential instability (Hope, 1995). She warned that "just as you wouldn't cross a busy street without looking, you shouldn't explore consciousness without precautions" (Hope, Chapter 1). This metaphor captures the essential tension of our current moment: psychic capacities are emerging faster than defensive knowledge can disseminate.

### The Threat Landscape

Both Hope and the PDI framework describe a populated, morally ambiguous psychic landscape where deception and manipulation are common. PDI identifies "deceivers and tricksters" who use fear, guilt, shame, lust, greed, and anger as tools of entrapment. Hope similarly describes "evil" energies as "misplaced/misdirected energy operating against cosmic law" characterized by selfishness and destructiveness (Hope, Chapter 1).

The specific threats identified across both frameworks include:

1. **Misguided or Malicious Entities**: Discarnate beings, elementals, and thought-forms that may attach to, influence, or attack individuals
2. **Psychic Attacks**: Persistent negative experiences manifesting as bad luck, health issues, mental fog, or emotional manipulation
3. **Open or Damaged Auras**: Vulnerability to intrusion, auric limpets, or psychic links
4. **Psychological Vulnerability**: Trauma, mental illness, or weak psychological boundaries as entry points
5. **Unprepared Exploration**: Dabbling without knowledge or protection

### The Psychic Defense Initiative Mission

The PDI's mission encompasses education, exploration of moral systems, and equipping individuals with tools for navigation. Its goals include:
- Educating people how to access psychic capacities without trauma
- Exploring and documenting moral systems encountered in the psychic realm
- Building affinity groups for collective security
- Establishing cognitive security (CogSec) as a foundation for psychic work

## Part One: Foundational Requirements

### Psychological and Physical Readiness

The prerequisites for safe psychic work include:

**A. Mental Health Foundation**
- Processing of trauma before engaging in psychic exploration
- Strong ego boundaries and sense of self
- Ability to distinguish between internal psychological content and external psychic phenomena
- No active mental illness or severe emotional instability
- Self-honesty about motivations and capabilities

**B. Physical Health and Brain Function**
- Clean food, water, and air
- Aerobic exercise for mitochondrial function
- Adequate sleep and rest
- Absence of artificial stimulants or drugs during psychic work
- Avoidance of alcohol at least 3 hours before practice (Hope, Chapter 13)

**C. Psychological Resources**
- Developed capacity for visualization
- Concentration and focus abilities
- Emotional regulation
- Discernment between imagination and perception

### Understanding the Psychic Anatomy

Hope provides a framework for understanding the energetic structures involved in psychic phenomena that are essential for defensive work.

**The Aura: First Line of Defense**

The aura is described as an "energy field protecting against disease, mental impingement, and energy loss" (Hope, Chapter 3). A damaged aura leads to illness and vulnerability. Hope describes how "discoloration or breaks" appear in the aura when a person is sick or under attack (Hope, Chapter 3).

The aura can be:
- **Strengthened**: Through visualization and conscious control
- **Masked**: Advanced practitioners can contract their aura to avoid detection
- **Sealed**: At the conclusion of psychic work to prevent intrusion

**The Mind-Brain Relationship**

Hope explains the crucial dualism: "mind/spirit uses brain as a tool" (Hope, Chapter 4). The subconscious relates to outer time and imagery, while consciousness operates through the brain's mechanisms. This understanding is essential for:
- Distinguishing between one's own thoughts and external intrusions
- Understanding how thought-forms can be created and dissolved
- Recognizing the relationship between brainwaves and psychic states

**The Chakras: Energy Vortices**

Hope describes seven energy vortices that "link subtle energies to physical body via endocrine glands" (Hope, Chapter 7). The chakras are points of potential vulnerability when imbalanced or overstimulated. Specific warnings include:
- **Throat chakra**: Imbalances cause expression issues
- **Ajna (third eye)**: Imbalances create clairvoyance distortion
- **Kundalini**: The serpent fire at the base of the spine must ascend carefully through each chakra

Hope's warning is stark: "Do not experiment with chakra healing without proper training" (Hope, Chapter 7).

### The Laws of Psychic Engagement

Hope articulates two fundamental laws governing psychic interaction that provide the foundation for defensive protocols:

**Law of Challenge**: "Place protection, challenge any presence before engaging. Varies by belief system" (Hope, Chapter 2). This law establishes the practitioner's sovereignty and agency, requiring explicit testing of any entity or energy encountered.

**Law of Equalities**: "In mental/energy battles, the stronger prevails. You cannot defeat a more advanced being with simple symbols" (Hope, Chapter 2). This law establishes that:
- True defense requires genuine development, not props
- Symbols are helpful but not sufficient
- The most powerful defense is one's own developed consciousness and connection to source

## Part Two: Core Defensive Techniques

### Aura Control and Strengthening

The foundational technique for psychic self-defense is developing conscious control over one's own aura. Hope describes this process as "like learning to ride a bike" (Hope, Chapter 3), requiring practice and persistence.

**Aura Visualization Protocol:**

1. **Basic Shielding**: Visualize stepping into a "blue/white plastic bag" or "cloak of light" that surrounds your entire body
2. **Color Selection**: 
   - **Safe**: White, blue, certain greens, pale lilac, gold, silver
   - **Avoid**: Red or brown around the head (associated with lower energies)
3. **Aura Strengthening**: Imagine your aura expanding to fill a room or space, then contracting again
4. **Masking**: For advanced practitioners, consciously contract the aura to avoid psychic detection

**Aura Examination for Vulnerability:**
- When physically or emotionally depleted, examine for "discoloration or breaks" in the aura
- Visualize repairing any damaged areas with white or blue light
- Practice daily aura reinforcement as preventive hygiene

### Symbolic and Ritual Protection

Hope establishes that "symbols satisfy devotional urge and tap into collective faith power" (Hope, Chapter 4). However, she warns against mixing incompatible systems as "incompatible rays can cause psychological issues" (Hope, Chapter 4).

**Universal Safety Symbols:**

| Symbol System | Safe Symbols |
|---------------|--------------|
| Christian | Cross, rose, chalice, dove |
| Egyptian | Ankh, Eye of Horus |
| Norse/Qabalistic | Specific runes, sephira |
| Eastern | Yin-yang, simple lotus |
| Pantheistic | Elemental symbols |

**Personal and God Symbols (Advanced):**

Hope provides a specific meditation journey for discovering one's personal symbols:

1. **Personal Symbol Discovery**: Meditate journeying back to conception, knotting a "silver cord" of life events, and rising to see the first image that appears
2. **God Symbol Discovery**: Visualize your personal symbol, rotate it clockwise, and meet your archetype (god/goddess, parental figure)
3. **Use Both Symbols**: For safe passage in altered states, use both personal and god symbols to maintain connection to group soul and divine source

**Creating a Protective Environment:**

- **Space Cleansing**: Extend light beam clockwise to cleanse room before work
- **Atmosphere Building**: Create psychic atmosphere aligned with your belief system
- **Color Psychology**: Use blues/greens for calm, gold for energy

### Mental Discipline and Visualization

Hope emphasizes that "thought precedes action in metaphysical realms" (Hope, Chapter 5). This principle underlies all defensive techniques and establishes the practitioner's ultimate power.

**Visualization for Protection:**

1. **Room Cleansing**: Visualize a light beam extending from you clockwise to cleanse all energy from the room
2. **Thought-Form Dissolution**: Advanced practitioners can dissolve unwanted thought-forms through concentrated visualization
3. **Shield Creation**: Create mental shields that follow specific patterns or symbols

**Emergency Protocols:**

Hope provides clear instructions for when things go wrong:
- "Return to normal consciousness, earth yourself, close aura, clear room again"
- "Call for higher help if needed" (Hope, Chapter 5)

**Thought-Form Policy:**
- Beginners should "avoid" creating thought-forms
- If created intentionally or accidentally, they must be properly dissolved
- Unused energy "attaches to the most psychic person, causing problems" (Hope, Chapter 11)

### Energy Control and Management

Hope emphasizes the importance of managing psychic energy before, during, and after any engagement.

**Pre-Work Preparation:**
- Avoid red meat, alcohol, or sex at least 3 hours before psychic work
- Strengthen the aura
- Invoke protective symbols
- Cleanse the space with light

**During Work:**
- Stay within your level of experience
- Use the Law of Challenge on any entity
- Maintain awareness and conscious control
- Monitor for discomfort or negative signs

**After Work:**
- "Properly 'earth' yourself (physical awareness exercises)" 
- "Close your aura, seal the space"
- "Direct any unused energy ethically (e.g., toward healing)" (Hope, Chapter 5)
- Use earthing exercises: tense/relax body parts from toes to head

## Part Three: Understanding and Countering Threats

### Types of Psychic Attack

Hope provides a comprehensive taxonomy of psychic threats that aligns with PDI's identification of deceivers and tricksters.

**Genuine Psychic Attack** is described as occurring when "mind encounters incompatible/hostile frequency from another intelligence/energy" (Hope, Chapter 6). However, Hope cautions that psychic attack is "often misdiagnosed" and may actually be "mental illness, suppressed emotions, or everyday stress" (Hope, Chapter 6).

**Forms of Attack Include:**

| Type | Description | Vulnerability |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Direct Attack | Mind-to-mind hostile energy | Open aura, weak boundaries |
| Thought-Form Attack | Encoded energy pockets from jealousy/hate | Emotional engagement with attacker |
| Entity Attachment | Auric limpets or spirits | Damage aura, open chakras |
| Astral Links | Possessive relationships | Strong emotional ties |
| Group Attack | Multiple attackers | Being known or targeted |

**Primary Symptoms of Attack:**
- Persistent bad luck or negative patterns
- Unexplained health issues
- Mental fog, confusion, or intrusive thoughts
- Emotional manipulation or destabilization
- Sleep disturbances or nightmares

### Countering Deceivers and Tricksters

PDI's description of deceivers and tricksters ("Evil Ones") identifies their tactics: "gain power from and are addicted to torturing others," "coerce individuals into doing evil, then hold it over your head," "pretend to be friends," "seduce people with psychic sex," and "boil down to bullying and intimidation" (PDI README, 2026).

Hope's framework for countering these threats includes:

**The Law of Challenge**: Always test entities before engagement. Formulate challenges that cannot be answered by simple lies.

**Frequencies and Vibrations**: "Raise vibration using mind power to create a 'fast' frequency that inhibits manifestation" (Hope, Chapter 11). Higher frequencies naturally repel lower, incompatible energies.

**Mental Transformation**: If pursued or attacked, "change form mentally" as in the Celtic tale of Keridwen and Gwion (Hope, Chapter 7).

**Boundary Maintenance**: 
- Politely ask spirits to refrain from manifesting (haunted house example)
- Use correct colors and symbols for your tradition
- Avoid ego-trips and attempts to dominate others

### Protecting Against Specific Vulnerabilities

**Trauma and Mental Health**
Hope and PDI agree that unresolved psychological issues increase vulnerability. Hope emphasizes that psychic work "requires strong, stable personality" and that "self-assessment is crucial before beginning" (Hope, Chapter 1). PDI emphasizes trauma processing as prerequisite.

**Drug Use**
Hope is unequivocal: "Drugs/alcohol damage brain cells, distort perceptions, and are unsafe for psychic work" (Hope, Chapter 6). This aligns with PDI's emphasis on clean living and cognitive security.

**Unstructured Meditation**
Hope warns that "open auras" can attract influences, and leaders must "seal auras post-session to prevent mental imbalance" (Hope, Chapter 6).

**Dabbling**
Hope's rule is absolute: "Always use safety measures when contacting unseen dimensions" (Hope, Chapter 5). Use of Ouija boards, planchettes, or unstructured exploration without protection invites unwanted influences.

**Incompatible Systems**
Hope's warning is clear: "Don't mix systems. Incompatible rays can cause psychological issues" (Hope, Chapter 4). Choose one tradition and study it deeply.

## Part Four: Protocols for Group Work

### The Leader's Responsibility

Hope places significant emphasis on the responsibilities of group leaders, establishing that they have "Karmic duty" to protect all participants (Hope, Chapter 5).

**Leader Duties:**
1. Protect all participants before work begins
2. Ensure everyone's aura is sealed after work
3. Direct group energy ethically (healing, peace, helping animals/ecology)
4. Conduct earthing exercises for all participants
5. Dissolve any negative thought-forms created during work

**Safety Protocol for Groups:**
- Establish the Law of Challenge at the beginning of every session
- Use a "caller/coverer system" for trance mediums
- Ensure no mixing of incompatible systems
- Monitor participants for signs of distress or imbalance
- Conclude every session with proper dismissal and earthing

### The Caller/Coverer System

Hope recommends the "caller/coverer system" as the safest approach to group work (Hope, Chapter 12). This involves:
- One person calls or invites the energy or entity
- Another person covers the first, projecting a protective field
- Both issue the Law of Challenge
- Ensure removed entities are sent to their rightful place

### Creating Thought-Forms as a Group

Hope warns that "accidental creation" of thought-forms occurs when "negative energy in a group forms an 'artificial elemental' that attaches to someone, causing discomfort/bad luck" (Hope, Chapter 11).

**Prevention:**
- Maintain positive, harmonious group energy
- Direct all generated power ethically
- Use earthing exercises to ground excess energy
- Dismiss all energies properly before session ends

**Dissolution if Created:**
"Group leader must use thought power/Law of Equalities to return energy to sender (with care)" (Hope, Chapter 11).

## Part Five: Ethical Frameworks and Cosmic Law

### The Nature of Good and Evil

Hope defines cosmic law as the "ordered pattern of the universe; harmonious when followed" (Hope, Chapter 1). Evil is "misplaced/misdirected energy operating against cosmic law; selfish/destructive" while good is "selfless/constructive" (Hope, Chapter 1).

This framework provides essential guidance for practitioners:

**Ethical Principles:**
1. Selflessness is constructive, selfishness is destructive
2. Love is universal and unconditional
3. Like attracts like—develop the qualities you wish to attract
4. Self-honesty is essential for development
5. Respect all consciousness—human, animal, elemental

### The Karmic Implications of Psychic Work

Hope establishes that practitioners have "ethical responsibility" and should not "perform readings/healings without proper skill" (Hope, Chapter 8). Key ethical guidelines include:

**Don'ts:**
- Don't tolerate demanding "guides" that disrupt sleep or health
- Don't ego-trip or take over others' sessions
- Don't condemn other belief systems
- Don't mix magical systems or instruments
- Don't experiment with advanced practices without proper training

**Dos:**
- Select a single path and study it deeply
- Keep an open mind, adjust views with new evidence
- Practice love, self-honesty, selflessness, and strength
- Recognize "old soul" traits: sensitivity, harmony, awareness of all life
- Aim for self-healing as the ultimate goal

## Part Six: Specialized Applications

### Dream Defense

Dreams represent a "link between inner/outer time" and "archetypal communication" (Hope, Chapter 7). They are both a vulnerability and a training ground.

**Dream Protection Protocol:**
1. Check normal causes for nightmares (diet, health) first
2. Use metaphysical clearing if needed (water, light, symbols)
3. Program awareness before sleep
4. Recognize dream state and dissolve threats with thought
5. Ask for divine protection before sleeping

### Astral Travel Defense

Hope distinguishes between "involuntary (sleep) and voluntary (conscious)" astral travel (Hope, Chapter 7).

**Astral Travel Protection:**
1. Program awareness and protective intent before travel
2. Use personal and god symbols for safe passage
3. If pursued, "change form mentally" (Hope, Chapter 7)
4. "Don't dabble alone; train with experienced practitioner" (Hope, Chapter 7)

### Psychic Reading Defense

Hope identifies that many "clairvoyants" simply read sitter's thoughts rather than accessing genuine psychic information (Hope, Chapter 8).

**Protection During Readings:**
1. Keep room clean physically and energetically
2. Build psychic atmosphere aligned with your belief
3. Detach emotionally; don't let clients overpower you
4. Use a "coverer" for trance mediums
5. Issue the Law of Challenge when appropriate

### Exorcism Protocols

Hope's warning is unequivocal: "Do not attempt unless highly trained" (Hope, Chapter 12). Misdiagnosis of mental illness versus possession "can be deadly" (Hope, Chapter 12).

**When Exorcism Is Appropriate:**
- Clear diagnosis of possession versus mental illness
- Highly trained practitioner only
- Caller/coverer system used
- Removed entity sent to rightful place
- Links and auric limpets severed carefully

**Safer Alternatives:**
- Sever links carefully, seal both auras
- Cleanse limpets with blue/white light
- Redirect lost spirits kindly to their own time zone/path via thought
