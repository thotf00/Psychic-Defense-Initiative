# Training Areas

The idea is to break down related skills \ specialties and create a training program to replace the "wild-west" of recruitment \ abusive practices.

In both the Aetheric (Lunar), and within the Mundane (Earthly) realms, there are various groups and organizations which focus on training in the Psychic arts.

These are also good ways to meet others seeking to for affinity groups, which individuals pool psychic resources for psychic defense. 

Each locale has a distributed set of recruitment practices for those showing talent to gain protection within the group and training in culture and practice.
