## Part I

### Chapter 1: The Yoga of the West and Its Roots
- Few students of occultism are familiar with the Western Tradition's origins
- Some scholars believe fragments are medieval forgeries due to intentional secrecy around initiation practices
- These fragments, supplemented by oral tradition and hidden manuscripts, form the basis of practical work in schools of initiation today

**The Origins of Western Mysticism**
- Rooted in Hebraic mysticism, which supplied foundation for modern Western occultism
- Theoretical basis for all ceremonial practices
- Tree of Life is a comprehensive meditation symbol and best representation we have

**The Influence of Judaism on European Spiritual Culture**
- Jews were the seedbearers of European spiritual culture due to their monotheistic beliefs
- Jesus and Paul, both Jewish, brought new dispensation grafted onto Judaic roots
- Other races lacked monotheistic traditions, making Judaism a necessary foundation for Western spirituality

**The Qabalah: Legitimate Descendants of Ancient Hebraic Mysticism**
- Modern Qabalistic teachings not identical with pre-Christian Rabbis but natural development therefrom
- Testing the purity of a tradition through reference to first principles and assessing its vitality by ability to assimilate contemporary thought

**Influences on Hebraic Mysticism**
- Chaldean star worshippers, Abraham's background in mysterious figure of Priest-king
- Interaction between Israelite princes and Egyptian priest-kings
- Influence from Tyrian Mysteries and Magi wisdom through Solomon, Hiram, Daniel, and Joseph

**Three Literatures of Ancient Hebraic Tradition**
- Old Testament: body of tradition
- Talmud: rational soul
- Qabalah: immortal spirit

**Jesus' Training in Essenian Community**
- Recognized caliber at a young age by learned doctors of the Law
- Sent to Essenes for mystical training before commencing mission at age 30.

#### Esoteric Roots of Christianity Qabalistic Influence and Mystical Traditions

**The Closing Clause of the Lord's Prayer and the Qabalah:**
- The closing clause of the Lord's Prayer is pure Qabalism
- Malkuth (The Kingdom), Hod (Glory), Netzach (Power), and Yesod (Foundation or Receptacle of Influences) form the basal triangle of the Tree of Life
- The person who formulated the prayer knew their Qabalah

**Christianity and Esotericism:**
- Christianity had an esoteric section in the Gnosis, which owed much to Greek and Egyptian thought
- Pythagoras adapted Qabalistic principles for Greek mysticism
- The exoteric Christian Church persecuted and destroyed esoteric literature
- Very little remains of our spiritual heritage on ancient wisdom, as most was destroyed during the excavation of Alexandria's library

**The Qabalah and Medieval Forgery:**
- It wasn't until the 15th century that scholars dared to commit the traditional Wisdom of Israel to paper
- The Qabalah is considered a medieval forgery because early manuscripts are not traced, but esoteric fraternities could convey knowledge through glyphs

**Mysticism and Yoga in Christianity:**
- Catholicism has rich treatises on mystical theology revealing practical acquaintance with higher states of consciousness
- However, Catholicism's Bhakti Yoga is suitable only for devotional temperaments
- Western aspirants often turn to Eastern methods, but they may not be as effective without proper guidance

**Recommendation for the Traditional Western System:**
- The author recommends the traditional Western system, which is admirably adapted to the psychic constitution of white races
- It gives immediate results and produces a unique vitality among adepts
- However, this system should only be practiced under proper supervision to avoid negative consequences when unsuitable systems are used

**The Thirty-Two Mystical Paths of Concealed Glory:**
- The Thirty-Two Mystical Paths are ways of life that must be tread by those who wish to unravel their secrets
- Training in these paths requires discipline and a willingness to undergo the necessary process.

### Chapter 2: The Choice of Path

**The Choice of Path**

**Flitting from System to System**:
- No student will make progress without committing to a system and practicing it thoroughly
- Each spiritual development method has its own value, but this value is realized by persevering with the practice

**Choosing a System**:
- To take occult studies seriously, one must choose a system and stick with it until achieving definite results or enhancing consciousness
- Once this is achieved, one may experiment with methods from other spiritual paths to build an eclectic technique and philosophy
- However, attempting to be an eclectic before becoming an expert will only result in dabbling

**Requirements of a Western Spiritual Development System**:
- The elementary technique must be readily grasped by undeveloped minds
- The forces brought to bear must be powerful enough to penetrate the relatively dense vehicles of the average Westerner
- The techniques must be suitable for modern individuals who have limited time to devote to spiritual practice
- These high psychic tensions cannot be sustained during the hard-driving life of a European city, so the techniques must be readily concentrated and dispersed

**Dharma and Civilization**:
- The racial dharma of the West is the conquest of dense matter, which differs from that of the East
- Applying Eastern methods to Western physiology can result in unforeseen or undesirable results
- The Western temperament demands life and seeks to conquer it, not to escape it

**The Role of Occult Powers**:
- The Western occultist seeks to concentrate the life-force, not simply to escape into spirit
- This White Magic involves using occult powers for spiritual ends

### Chapter 3: The Method of the Qabalah

**The Qabalistic Method**

**Background**
- Ancient Jewish mystical system called the Tree of Life
- Attempts to diagrammatically represent every force and factor in the manifested universe and human soul
- Originated from angels, beings of another order of creation than humanity
- Comparative religions agree that Divine beings teach those with spiritual experience

**Approach of the Qabalist**
- Opposite to natural science: analyzes abstract concepts instead of building synthetic ones
- Uses a symbolic diagram (Tree of Life) to understand abstract reality, rather than metaphysical theories
- Symbols used as concrete representations, like algebraic equations, to guide thought into the unseen and incomprehensible

**Symbolism in Meditation**
- Uninitiated use symbols for concentration and emotional stimulation
- Initiate uses a composite symbol (Tree of Life) to observe relationships between parts
- Mind traverses distances between known and unknown by contemplating symbols
- Symbols help build concepts, but unthinkable things are initially incapable of being fully grasped

**The Qabalistic System**
- Not a historic monument, but a growing system as mental content becomes richer with ideas
- Symbols precede their elucidation; the Qabalah is an evolving system.

#### The Qabalistic Tree of Life A Symbolic Representation of Cosmic Forces and Universal Consciousness

**The Sphere of the Moon and the Tree of Life**
- The **Sphere of the Moon** is connected to other Sephiroth through Paths
- Biological Qabalist explores relationships between forces in Yesod and symbols on the Tree of Life
- Each symbol can be interpreted on different planes and related to gods of various pantheons
- Symbols evoke images in the mind, following well-defined association tracks in the Universal Mind

**The Universe as a Thought Form**
- The universe is a thought-form projected from the mind of God
- The Qabalistic Tree represents the hidden forces and processes that led to the creation of the universe
- The Tree applies not only to the Macrocosm but also to the Microcosm, making divination possible

**Soul and Consciousness**
- The soul of man and the universe are connected through correspondences arising from developmental identities
- Subconscious connection between individual souls and the World-soul allows sharing in cosmic tides
- Each symbol on the Tree represents a cosmic force or factor, allowing access to that force through meditation

**The Importance of Balance**
- The universe must be ruled by God, and the human soul by its Higher Self (the spirit of man)
- Unbalanced forces within the soul can result in conflict between different factors, leading to "the rule of the Kings of Edom"

**Chapter 4: The Unwritten Qabalah**

**Approach to Holy Qabalah**:

**Interpretation of Qabalah**:
- Not found among Rabbinical authorities (Outer Israel)

**Author's Approach**:
- Based on modern Qabalistic practice for spiritual development
- Uses works of predecessors as a quarry to build upon
- Frank acknowledgment of deficiencies, but confident in value of system

**Scholarship and Qualifications**:

**Classification of Qabalah**:

**Unwritten Qabalah**:
- Received by MacGregor Mathers from occult teachers in 1887

#### Exploring Qabalah Symbolism and Manifestations of Sephiroth on the Tree of Life

**The Unwritten Qabalah: Understanding the System and Symbols**

**Background:**
- Rare books by scholarly esotericists, valuable but hard to find
- System based on Tree of Life (Ets Chayyim) with Ten Holy Sephiroth and thirty-two paths
- Ancient traps for uninitiated: twenty-two Paths on the Tree instead of thirty-two
  - Rabbis misled by associating Ten Sephiroth as Paths

**Modern Authorities:**
- MacGregor Mathers, Wynn Westcott, A.E. Waite (principal modern authorities)
- Differences in understanding of the system
- Crowley's system considered correct based on final test and fitting accurately with Tarot trumps

**Symbols:**
- Twenty-two letters of Hebrew alphabet associated with Paths without discrepancy or overlapping
- Twenty-two Tarot trumps, Atus, or Abodes of Thoth also connected to the system
- Three modern authorities: Dr. Encausse (Papus), Mr. A.E. Waite, MacGregor Mathers' Order of the Golden Dawn MS

**Unwritten Qabalah:**
- Essence lies in understanding order of symbols arranged on Tree of Life
- Twenty-three symbols: Signs of Zodiac, Planets, Elements (excluding Earth)
- Solution to fitting these symbols on twenty-two Paths is a blind or trap for uninitiated

**Worlds:**
- Ten Holy Sephiroth have fourfold nature: Atziluth, Briah, Yetzirah, Assiah
  - Each World manifests Divine Emanations differently (Names of God, Archangels, Angelic Hosts)

**Sephirotic Standpoint:**
- Lower Astral and Etheric Planes form background of matter in the Assiatic World.

#### Qabalistic Wisdom Exclusivity vs Accessibility

**The Sephiroth and Paths of the Tree of Life**
- The **Sephiroth** represent macrocosmic, creative emanations in the universe
- The **Paths** represent microcosmic stages of cosmic realization within human consciousness
- The serpent Nechushtan, symbolizing wisdom and initiation, is often depicted twined around the Tree of Life's branches
- The coils of this serpent indicate the order in which the Paths should be numbered
- Correctly arranging the symbols on the Tree allows for accurate representation of the Sephiroth and Paths
- Some modern books withhold this information, potentially to maintain a monopoly on knowledge

**Accessibility to Qabalistic Knowledge**
- Information once considered secret can be found in various occult literature
- Much of this information has already been published by notable figures like Mathers and Westcott
- The concepts originated from biblical texts and medieval Rabbis, suggesting the information is no longer under copyright
- Ultimately, the "ownership" of these ideas lies with their original authors, not subsequent commentators

**Critique of Secrecy in Occult Knowledge**
- Some initiates confine knowledge under an oath of secrecy
- Critics argue this exclusivity goes against the spirit of openness and accessibility
- **The Early Fathers** were criticized for dismissing the essential keys to understanding scripture, which are found in Qabalistic Cosmology

### Chapter 6: ETS CHAYYIM, the Tree of Life

**Chapter 6: The Tree of Life (ETS CHAYYIM)**

**Understanding the ETS CHAYYIM:**
- Represents the cosmos and soul of man
- A glyph for scanning and calculating existence
- Consists of ten circles (Sephiroth) and twenty-two lines (Paths)

**General Layout and Significance:**
- Arranged in three vertical columns
- Each circle represents a phase of evolution (Sephirah)
- Lines connect the Sephiroth, representing subjective consciousness (Paths)

**Kether (The Crown):**
- Represents the first manifestation of reality
- Apex of topmost triangle of Sephiroth
- Not a physical location but a state of existence

**Symbolism:**
- Primal Point or Centre of Manifestation
- Concealed of the Concealed, Inscrutable Height

**Comparison with Exoteric Judaism:**
- Qabalah conceives God as evolving creation
- Christianity inherited this limitation from Judaism
- Exoteric Judaism does not contain concept of Emanations or overflowing Sephiroth
- Old Testament writers were familiar with the Qabalistic tradition, but it was not explicitly taught in their texts.

**Importance:**
- Understanding the Tree of Life is crucial for grasping individual Sephiroth's significance.

#### Evolution of Sephiroth Kether to Binah through Overflowing Phases

**The Unmanifest and Manifest**
- **Kether**: Fountain that fills its basin, overflow feeds another fountain (Chokmah)
- Overflow from Kether represents a change in mode of existence
  - Crystallization of simpler form into complex structures (Chokmah)
  - Beginning of organic life

**The Three Sephiroth**
1. **Kether**: Unmanifest, source of manifestation, pure form of existence
   * Essence of manifestation
   * Influences of preceding phases are external and reflected
2. **Chokmah (Second Sephirah)**: Overflow from Kether
   * Complex mode of existence with no life maintenance possible
   * Forerunner of organic life, called Mazzaloth or Sphere of the Zodiac
3. **Binah (Third Sephirah)**: Overflow from Chokmah
   * Triangular figure on Tree of Life called Triangle of the Three Supernals or First Trinity
   * Roots of existence hidden from normal human consciousness

**Paths and Diagram**
- Paths pursued by Emanations in successive overflows represented by Lightning Flash or Flaming Sword on Tree of Life diagram I.

### Chapter 7: The Three Supernals

**Background:**
- In this chapter, we delve deeper into the nature of the first Divine Emanations or Sephiroth

- Meaning: Understanding

- Also called Ama, the Supernal Mother, the Great Sea, Marah, Aima, and the Bright Fertile Mother

**Daath (Knowledge):**
- Invisible Sephirah situated astride the Abyss

#### Understanding Binah and Daath in Qabalistic Meditation

**Binah: The Principle of Form**
- Binah is the third sephirah in the Kabbalistic Tree of Life
- Represents the idea of Form and gives rise to the basic form or plan of the Divine Intention
- Attributed to the Mother Goddess, Jehovah's Feminine Power
- Binah represents the Aspect of God that brings force into outer manifestation
- Gives mental form to highest ideas and can give crudest shapes or symbols to lowest earthly forces
- Form is a limitation but necessary for force to manifest in the Solar Universe

**Daath: The Point of Unity**
- Daath is not a sephirah but an important point on the Tree of Life
- Represents unity and unification of all knowledge, Law, Justice, and Equilibrium
- Holds the key to man's knowledge of himself
- Takes inner development to use the key effectively
- In pre-Christian times, connected with Beltane festival and Creative Fire in the realm of mind.

#### Symbols and Correspondences in the Sphere of Daath

**Daath: The Knowledge Sephirah**

**Title**: Daath (Daleth, Ayin, Tau in Hebrew)

**Gods**: Horus, Pallas Athene, Janus, Balder

**Angels**: The Four Mighty Archangels of the Quarters

**Mythological Figures**: Prometheus, Galahad, Perseus and The Heroes (Sons of the Immortals)

**Star**: Sirius

**Virtues**: Detachment, Perfection of Justice, Application of Virtues untainted by personality considerations

**Confidence in the Future**

**Colours of Daath**: Various colours associated with this Sphere, but not ordinary terrestrial colours or human standards. Unusual red (Supernal Justice), green, speckled brown and white, shade of blue like electric flame, lavender, silvery grey, pure violet, grey flecked with yellow.

**Vices and Dangers**: Doubt of the future, apathy, inertia, cowardice (fear of the future), pride leading to isolation and disintegration

**Symbols**: The Empty Room; The Condemned Cell; The Prism; A high Mountain Peak; The Grain of Corn.

**The Empty Room Symbolism**: Absence of symbol and contact with reality, preparation for final contact with Daath, absorption into consciousness, synthesis of past and future in the present.

**Abyss**: Indication of difference in Spirit's functioning above vs below the Abyss (love, wisdom, strength vs sensations, emotions, feelings, mind). Consciousness of Daath not achieved through abnegation but extension and change of these factors.
