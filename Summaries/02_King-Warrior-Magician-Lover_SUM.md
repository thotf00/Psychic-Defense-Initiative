**The King Archetype of Mature Masculinity**

**The King**:
- Primal energy found in all men

- Similar to the Divine Child archetype but more wise and complex
- Represents the primordial man, Adam, or Anthropos in each man

- Historically, kings were sacred and embodied generative power

**Transition to Manhood**:
- The Hero (adolescent boy) "dies" when the King energy comes online

---

**The King Archetype: Organizing and Healing**

**Background:**
- Ancient mythology identifies the King as the "central archetype" around which psyche is organized.
- The King represents order, creativity, and healing.

**Functions of the King:**
1. **Ordering**:
   - Defines reality through words and concepts
   - Radiates outward from a central point
   - Geometrically organizes the world into four quarters
2. **Providing Fertility and Blessing:**
   - Reigns over ordered world
   - Locus of divine revelation and creative power
3. **Symbolism**:
   - Seen in ancient mythology, art, and maps
   - Appears in dreams and paintings during psychoanalysis (mandalas)
4. **Human Kings**:
   - Embody ordering principle of the Divine World
   - Receive laws from the King energy to maintain peace and calm
5. **Examples:**
   - Ancient Egypt: Ptah, Yahweh
   - Babylonian civilization: Hammurabi and Shamash
6. **Impact**:
   - Transitions from Boy psychology to Man psychology
   - Renewal, healing, reorganization of the personality

1. Receives and embodies right order for his people
2. Lives according to Dharma, Tao, or Ma'at
3. Maintains societal order through rule and example
4. Defends against chaos (Warrior function)
5. Fertility and blessing: associates with creative ordering of things by gods

**King's Role in Bestowing Blessing**
